The Charlie McNeil Memorial Pairs Tournament 2025

Polmaise Bowling Club was proud to host this year's Annual Charles McNeil Memorial Open Pairs Tournament on August 16, 2025. The day brought together friendship, competition, and community spirit in honour of a much-loved figure in our club's history.

With 16 pairs taking to the green, the standard of bowling was excellent throughout, and the sun certainly added to the occasion – with roasting conditions making it a memorable day for everyone involved.

Tournament Winners: George Rough & Gary Stevens

Runners-Up: Alexander Reynolds & Jamie Sneddon
