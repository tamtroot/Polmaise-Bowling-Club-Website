                                <p>Spring is finally on the horizon, and at Polmaise Bowling Club that can only mean one thing — the green is about to open and the bowls bags are coming back out of hiding.</p>

                                <p>After a long winter hiatus, the green will officially open on Saturday 4th April 2026, marking the start of another busy and sociable bowling season at Polmaise. Judging by the cartoon above, many of our members have been waiting patiently (or not so patiently!) for this moment — bags packed, smiles on faces, and partners already resigned to seeing a little less of them over the coming months.</p>

                                <p>The opening day is always a special occasion in the club calendar, and this year is no exception. We're delighted that several of our valued sponsors will be making an appearance, and the club is keen to ensure they're welcomed by a strong turnout from the membership. Their continued support plays a vital role in keeping the club thriving, and opening day is the perfect opportunity to show our appreciation.</p>

                                <p>As ever, the emphasis will be on good company, friendly conversation, and that unmistakable sense of anticipation that comes with the first roll of the season. Whether you're a regular on the green, returning after a winter break, or simply keen to soak up the atmosphere, all members are encouraged to attend.</p>

                                <p>So dust off the whites, locate the bowls bag, and prepare for the familiar seasonal farewell to your partner — "See you in five months!" We look forward to welcoming everyone back to the green and kicking off what promises to be another enjoyable year at Polmaise.</p>

                                <p>For more information on this years fixtures check out: <a href="https://polmaisebowlingclub.com/fixtures.html" target="_blank">https://polmaisebowlingclub.com/fixtures.html</a></p>
