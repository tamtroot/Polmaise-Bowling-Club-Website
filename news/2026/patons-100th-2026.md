<div class="news-content">
                                <p>Polmaise Bowling Club were delighted to visit Patons &amp; Baldwins Bowling Club as part of their 100th anniversary celebrations for their annual friendly fixture.</p>

                                <p>The match was played in great spirit at Patons &amp; Baldwins' green, with both clubs enjoying a competitive and friendly day of bowls. Polmaise came out on top overall, winning by 59 shots to 55, but the day was about much more than the final score.</p>

                                <p>Polmaise would like to congratulate Patons &amp; Baldwins Bowling Club on reaching their 100th year and wish the club, their members, President and Vice President a fantastic anniversary season.</p>

                                <p>The rink scores were as follows (Patons and Baldwins skips first):</p>

                                <ul class="plain-list">
                                    <li>S. Franklye 15–17 D. Dalgleish</li>
                                    <li>H. Spence 18–12 R. Donaldson</li>
                                    <li>J. Buckley 17–13 P. M. Black</li>
                                    <li>D. Ballingham 7–17 T. Stevenson</li>
                                </ul>

                                <p><strong>Final Score: Patons 55 – 59 Polmaise</strong></p>

                                <p>As part of the occasion, Polmaise's Vice President Robert Donaldson presented Patons President H. Spence with a commemorative engraved glass plaque from Polmaise Bowling Club to mark the club's centenary year.</p>

                                <p>It was a pleasure for Polmaise to be part of such a special milestone, and everyone at the club hopes Patons &amp; Baldwins have a memorable and successful 100th anniversary year.</p>

                                <p>Well done to everyone who took part and helped make the day such an enjoyable occasion.</p>
