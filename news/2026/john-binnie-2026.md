                                <p>Polmaise Bowling Club was delighted to renew its friendly match with Spittalmyre Bowling Club after a number of years, in an occasion made even more special by the presentation of a trophy in remembrance of John Binnie.</p>

                                <p>John's sons, John and Scott, joined us on the day to present the trophy to the winners, adding a fitting and personal touch to a memorable afternoon for everyone involved.</p>

                                <p>There was plenty of good bowling on show, with Polmaise putting in a strong all-round performance across the rinks.</p>

                                <h4>Match Results</h4>

                                <p>Polmaise skips first</p>

                                <ul class="plain-list">
                                    <li>D. Stewart 12 v 7 G. Dawson</li>
                                    <li>T. Stevenson 16 v 9 M. Reid</li>
                                    <li>P. M. Black 16 v 6 F. Gray</li>
                                    <li>L. Begg 11 v 11 S. Stewart</li>
                                    <li>R. Donaldson 12 v 7 N. Hatton</li>
                                </ul>

                                <p><strong>Final Score: Polmaise 67 shots – Spittalmyre 40 shots</strong></p>

                                <p>A big thank you goes to everyone who took part and helped make the day such a success. Thanks also to the bar staff, Ronda and Mag for the buffet, and the ladies for the baking.</p>

                                <p>It was a day of good company, good bowling and warm remembrance — a fine occasion for both clubs and Polmaise wishes Spittalmyre Bowling Club a successful season.</p>
