<div class="news-content">
                                <p>Saturday was a very special day at Polmaise Bowling Club as the club hosted a Hamilton Trophy fixture and celebrated a remarkable milestone for one of its most decorated players, Sandy Deans.</p>

                                <p>The occasion marked Sandy's 300th county cap, an outstanding achievement and a reflection of his long-standing dedication, consistency and contribution to the game of bowls.</p>

                                <p>To recognise the milestone, Sandy was given a guard of honour by fellow bowlers as he entered the green. It was a fitting tribute to a player who has represented both club and county with distinction over many years.</p>

                                <p>As part of the celebrations, Sandy was also presented with a special commemorative watch.</p>

                                <p>Sandy has been at the heart of many proud moments for Polmaise Bowling Club and continues to be one of the club's most respected figures. Reaching 300 county caps is a rare and impressive achievement, and everyone at Polmaise was delighted to see the occasion marked in such a memorable way.</p>

                                <p>After the event, Sandy shared a heartfelt message of thanks:</p>

                                <blockquote class="historical-quote">
                                    "I have had one of the best days of my life today. Polmaise Bowling Club looked after me so well and made me proud to be a member of this great club. They showed exactly how a special Hamilton Trophy county game should be done.<br><br>
                                    Thank you from Sandy and Linda."
                                </blockquote>

                                <p>Everyone at Polmaise Bowling Club would like to congratulate Sandy on this fantastic achievement.</p>

                                <p>300 county caps, 40 years of dedication, and a day to remember.</p>

                                <p>Congratulations, Sandy — a true Polmaise great.</p>
