<div class="news-content">
                                <p>Polmaise Bowling Club is delighted to congratulate <strong>Aaron Furzer</strong>, who has won the <strong>Stirling County East &amp; West Champion of Champions</strong> title.</p>

                                <p>Organised by the County Association, the Champion of Champions competition brings together the <strong>Gents Singles champions from clubs throughout the Stirling East and Stirling West county districts</strong>, with each player earning their place by first winning their own club singles championship.</p>

                                <p>Representing Polmaise, Aaron progressed through the competition to reach the Grand Final at <strong>Grangemouth Bowling Club</strong>, where he faced <strong>Gary Stirling</strong> in an excellent contest between two in-form players.</p>

                                <p>Aaron made a strong start to the match and began to take control in the early stages, moving into a <strong>7&ndash;2 lead after seven ends</strong>. He continued to bowl well through the middle of the game and had built a <strong>13&ndash;7 advantage after 15 ends</strong>.</p>

                                <p>Gary battled back well and reduced the gap to <strong>17&ndash;14</strong> with three ends remaining, but Aaron responded superbly under pressure. A vital single on the 22nd end helped steady the position before Aaron produced a decisive finish, picking up <strong>three shots on the 24th end to seal a 21&ndash;15 victory</strong> and secure the title.</p>

                                <p>It was a performance that showed not only Aaron&rsquo;s quality, but also his composure at key stages of the match.</p>

                                <p>The achievement is made even more impressive by the fact that Aaron is <strong>one of the youngest players competing at this level</strong>. To come through a field made up of club singles champions from across both county districts and lift such a prestigious title is a tremendous accomplishment and a proud moment for both Aaron and <strong>Polmaise Bowling Club</strong>.</p>

                                <p>Club President <strong>Davie Stewart</strong> was delighted to share in the celebrations afterwards, with Aaron proudly returning to Polmaise with the trophy.</p>

                                <p>The club would also like to congratulate <strong>Gary Stirling</strong> on an excellent final and thank <strong>Grangemouth Bowling Club</strong>, along with everyone involved in organising and hosting the competition.</p>
