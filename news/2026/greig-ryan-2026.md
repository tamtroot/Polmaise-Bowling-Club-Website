<div class="news-content">
                                <p>Polmaise Bowling Club was delighted to host the 2026 Greig Ryan Pairs competition, with a full entry taking to the green for a day of competitive bowls played in excellent spirit.</p>

                                <p>The weather was kind throughout the day, providing perfect conditions for an event that ran smoothly from start to finish. Players, supporters, and spectators came together to remember Greg Ryan, a former member of the club's junior section, making the competition a fitting tribute to his memory.</p>

                                <p>After a series of closely contested matches, Aaron Furzer and Sandy Deans emerged as the 2026 champions, producing an impressive performance throughout the tournament to lift the Greig Ryan Pairs trophy.</p>

                                <p>The runners-up were K. Patterson and Gary Malcolmson, who battled their way to the final before narrowly missing out on the title.</p>

                                <p>Reaching the semi-final stage were the pairs of A. Edmonds &amp; D. Wallace and C. Carderry &amp; A. Brown, both of whom played superbly throughout the day and can be proud of their achievements.</p>

                                <p>Polmaise Bowling Club would like to thank everyone who took part, attended, volunteered, and helped make the event such a success. Special thanks also go to John Ryan, Greg's father, for joining us on the day and presenting the trophy to the winners.</p>

                                <p>Congratulations once again to Aaron Furzer and Sandy Deans on becoming the 2026 Greig Ryan Pairs Champions.</p>
