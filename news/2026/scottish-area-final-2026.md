                                <p>It was an evening of high stakes, fantastic bowls, and tremendous club pride as Polmaise Bowling Club representatives took to the pristine greens at Menstrie Bowling Club for the Scottish Area Bowls Final. Competing for the Stirling and Clackmannanshire Districts titles, our players delivered performances that will be remembered for years to come against the picturesque backdrop of the Ochil Hills.</p>

                                <h4>Singles Success: Craig and Furzer Claim Gold</h4>
                                <p>The spotlight shone brightly on our singles competitors, who both secured magnificent victories to punch their tickets to the National Finals.</p>

                                <p>In the Under 25 Singles, James Craig showcased incredible focus and skill, overcoming a tough opponent, D. Kirk from Clackmannan Bowling Club. His precise drawing and composure under pressure earned him a well-deserved victory, finishing with a commanding 21-13 scoreline.</p>

                                <p>Matching this success in the Gents Singles, Aaron Furzer delivered a dramatic performance against K. Wallace from Alloa East End. After trailing for the majority of the game, Aaron showed incredible resilience and pushed hard in the final ends to seize the lead, ultimately claiming a stunning 21-19 victory.</p>

                                <h4>Valiant Effort in the Gents Pairs</h4>
                                <p>In the Gents Pairs, James Brown and Gary Malcomson fought a brilliant and tightly contested battle against a strong duo from Alloa East End. Despite displaying excellent teamwork and making some superb shots throughout the match, they were ultimately edged out, ending the game with a 12-18 score. Though defeated, James and Gary represented Polmaise with exceptional grit and sportsmanship, and the club is immensely proud of their journey to the Area Finals.</p>

                                <h4>Next Stop: Northfield, Ayr</h4>
                                <p>Following their fantastic triumphs, both James Craig and Aaron Furzer will now proudly go on to represent Polmaise Bowling Club at the prestigious Bowls Scotland National Finals at Northfield, Ayr. This is a monumental achievement for the players and a proud moment for our club. We encourage all members to get behind the lads and support them as they take on the best in the country!</p>
