<div class="news-content">
                            <p>Polmaise Bowling Club can proudly announce a major victory in the 2025 City of Stirling Top Fifteen tournament, securing the title with a thrilling final win on Sunday, 10 August 2025. This well-earned triumph reflects the club's skill, determination, and unwavering team spirit.</p>

                            <h4>The Winning Journey</h4>
                            <h5>Round One (3 July 2025)</h5>
                            <p>Polmaise opened their campaign with a decisive win over Doune Castle BC, advancing comfortably to the quarter-finals.</p>

                            <h5>Quarter-Final (10 July 2025)</h5>
                            <p>In a compelling match, Polmaise outplayed Bridge of Allan BC to book their place in the semi-finals.</p>

                            <h5>Semi-Final (17 July 2025)</h5>
                            <p>At home, Polmaise delivered a commanding performance against Cambusbarron BC to reach the final.</p>

                            <h5>Final (10 August 2025)</h5>
                            <p>Facing Causewayhead BC, Polmaise maintained their momentum and secured a decisive victory, clinching the championship.</p>
