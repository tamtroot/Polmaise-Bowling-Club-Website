<div class="news-content">
                            <p>We are delighted to announce the launch of a special historical archive of Polmaise Bowling Club, made possible by the generous contribution of our long-standing member, Sandy Deans.</p><br>

                            <p>Sandy has kindly handed over his personal collection of photographs and newspaper clippings, which he has gathered and preserved from the early 1980s right through to recent times. This remarkable archive provides a unique and vivid record of the club's journey over the past four decades.</p><br>

                            <p>The collection captures the evolution of Polmaise Bowling Club, documenting everything from memorable matches and championship wins to the many familiar faces and moments that have shaped our club's story.</p><br>

                            <p>A dedicated page has been created on our website to showcase this archive. </p><br>

                            <p>We extend our heartfelt thanks to Sandy Deans for entrusting us with this treasured archive and for his outstanding dedication to preserving and celebrating the heritage of Polmaise Bowling Club.</p><br>
