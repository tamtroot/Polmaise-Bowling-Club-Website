<div class="news-content">
                            <p>Polmaise Bowling Club was proud to host this year's Annual Charles McNeil Memorial Open Pairs Tournament, a day that brought together friendship, competition, and community spirit in honour of a much-loved figure in our club's history.</p>

                            <p>With 16 pairs taking to the green, the standard of bowling was excellent throughout, and the sun certainly added to the occasion – with roasting conditions making it a memorable day for everyone involved.</p>
