                            <p>Spittalmyre Bowling Club played host to an exciting evening of competition on Tuesday 24th June, as Polmaise's finest took to the green in the Scottish District Qualifier Finals.</p>

                            <div class="news-image news-image-spaced">
                                {% photoImage "../../../Images/2025/ScottishQ/20250624_193855.jpg", "Gary Malcolmson in action during the District Singles Final", { loading: "lazy", sizes: "(max-width: 768px) 90vw, 375px", class: "zoomable-image" } %}
                            </div>

                            <p>Gary Malcolmson, who had battled his way to the final of the District Singles Qualifying round, put in a strong performance but was ultimately defeated 21–16 by Kevin Frogly of Borestone Bowling Club. While the result didn't go Gary's way this time, his run to the final has been a fantastic achievement and he can hold his head high.</p>

                            <p>Meanwhile, in the District Pairs Final, Aaron Furzer and William Black delivered a dominant performance against father and son duo Alan and Robert Hunter Snr of Alva. With precision and teamwork, the Polmaise pair secured a convincing victory of 22-6 with 1 end left to play and now advance to the Scottish National Championships in Ayr later this summer.</p>

                            <p>Gary's son, Kyle Malcolmson, played in the Scottish Under 25 Singles Qualifier Final on Monday 30th June at Spittalmyre, facing Robert Hunter Jnr of Alva. Despite a fantastic effort, Kyle was defeated 21–11 by a very experienced opponent. He can be proud of his performance and will no doubt bounce back stronger.</p>

                            <p>Congratulations to all our players for their efforts and dedication. The Club is proud of your achievements, and we look forward to seeing more success on the greens.</p>
