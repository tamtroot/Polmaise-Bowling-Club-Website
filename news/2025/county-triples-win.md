                            <p>Polmaise Bowling Club is celebrating another remarkable success as our triples team clinched the County Championship Triples title in commanding fashion.</p>

                            <p>The formidable trio of Gary Malcomson (Skip), Stephen Fleming (Second), and William Black (Lead) faced off against a strong team from Stenhousemuir—Aaron McFarlane (Skip), Alan McFarlane (Second), and Stuart Williamson (Lead). However, the Polmaise side proved too strong on the day, producing a dominant performance and sealing the victory with an end to spare.</p>

                            <p>This outstanding win reflects the dedication, skill, and teamwork of the Polmaise players, and adds yet another trophy to the club's growing list of honours.</p>

                            <p>In the County Championship Singles, Aaron Furzer represented Polmaise with great distinction, narrowly missing out on victory in a tightly contested match against Jim Aitken of Allandale. Arron was just edged out 21-18, showing resilience and class in a thrilling encounter.</p>

                            <p>The club would like to congratulate Gary, Stephen, William, and Arron for their performances and for continuing to fly the Polmaise flag high in County competition.</p>

                            <p>Well done, gents! 💙🏆</p>
