                                        <h4>POLMAISE TOP OF THE POPS!</h4>

                                        <!-- Original newspaper clipping in square format -->
                                        <div class="historical-inline-image">
                                            {% photoImage "../../../Images/Archive/HistoricalArchive/Stirling_Observer_17_June_1988_0022_Clip.jpg", "Original newspaper clipping of Polmaise Top of the Pops", { loading: "lazy", sizes: "(max-width: 768px) 90vw, 375px", class: "zoomable-image historical-clipping-image" } %}

                                        <p>Polmaise Bowling Club were the pride of Stirlingshire on Sunday when they became Scottish Top Ten Champions. In the finals of the inter-county tournament in Edinburgh they won the title with a fine win over Bonnyrigg Bowling Club by six points to two.</p>

                                        <p>The final took place on the Blairfield green and the scores were:</p>

                                        <ul class="plain-list--inset">
                                            <li><strong>Singles</strong> - Polmaise (J. Blevins) 21, Bonnyrigg 19</li>
                                            <li><strong>Pairs</strong> - Polmaise (S. Deans and P. Black) 25, Bonnyrigg 10</li>
                                            <li><strong>Triples</strong> - Polmaise (J. Walker, W. Bone and R. Fulton) 15, Bonnyrigg 14</li>
                                            <li><strong>Fours</strong> - Polmaise (H. Scott, J. Bone, A. Buchanan and J. Black) 5, Bonnyrigg 19</li>
                                        </ul>

                                        <p>Earlier in the day, Polmaise disposed of Headwell Bowling Club in the semi-final played on Gorgie Mills green.</p>

                                        <p>So the conquering heroes returned to Fallin on Sunday evening as Scottish Champions, bringing honour to the club, the village, and the County.</p>

                                        <p>Congratulations Polmaise!</p>
