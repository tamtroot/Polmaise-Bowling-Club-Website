                                        <h4>SILVERWARE FOR RITA AND GARY</h4>

                                        <!-- Original newspaper clipping in square format -->
                                        <div class="historical-inline-image">
                                            {% photoImage "../../../Images/Archive/HistoricalArchive/Gary&Rita.jpg", "Original newspaper clipping of Rita and Gary's championship win", { loading: "lazy", sizes: "(max-width: 768px) 90vw, 375px", class: "zoomable-image historical-clipping-image" } %}

                                        <p>There were celebrations in the Stirling area last week as two outstanding Polmaise bowlers collected national silverware. Rita Smith and Gary Malcolmson were in dazzling form on the sun-drenched Northfield greens in Ayr to become the Bowls Scotland mixed pairs champions.</p>

                                        <p>The Polmaise duo clinched the Scottish title on Thursday afternoon with a highly convincing 18-4 victory over the Sandhead club from Stranraer - with an amazing three ends to spare!</p>

                                        <p>Earlier wins over Calderbank (20-16), Langside (20-12), Hillside (18-16) and Springside (15-10) put Rita and Gary into the final where they completely blew away their south of Scotland opponents.</p>

                                        <p>Their national success was the Stirling district's first since 2002 when Polmaise triples triumphed on the same stage.</p>
